asked a lot

Questions before you apply.

I don't know anyone. Is that weird?

That's the whole point. Almost everyone comes alone. The bus shuffle, the Room Draft teams and the captains are built so you're talking to new people within the first hour.

Can I come with a friend?

Yes, both of you apply separately and mention each other. You'll end up on different Room Draft teams, which is half the fun.

How hard is the Raghupur Fort trek?

About 3 km each way from Jalori Pass, mostly gentle with a few steep bits, and 2 to 3 hours with breaks. If you can climb a few flights of stairs without stopping, you're fine. Anyone who'd rather skip it can stay at the pass with a captain.

What if Jalori Pass is closed?

In late December snow often closes the pass road. We check 48 hours before departure. If it's shut, Day 2 becomes the Chehni Kothi trek and a Tirthan riverside afternoon, at no extra cost.

How cold does it get?

In December, nights in Jibhi go near freezing and Jalori goes well below it. Pack a proper jacket and thermals. The pack list above is the minimum.

What happens after I apply?

You pay the slot fee to enter screening. A captain checks your socials and calls you. If selected, you pay the balance before departure. If not, your slot fee is refunded in full.
